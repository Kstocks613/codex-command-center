# Onboarding Questions

Ask only what is required to unblock work.

## Critical Blockers

1. What are we building?
2. Who is it for?
3. What does success look like?
4. What existing repo/files/assets should I use?
5. What can I change without asking?
6. What must I always ask before changing?
7. What accounts/APIs already exist?
8. Where should this deploy?
9. What is the first useful deliverable?
10. What should I explicitly not build yet?
