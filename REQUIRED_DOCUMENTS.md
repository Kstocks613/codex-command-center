## Documentation / Asset Request Checklist

Ask the user for relevant:

- Product notes, briefs, PRDs, requirements, or strategy docs.
- URLs for references, competitors, APIs, docs, or existing products.
- Screenshots, mockups, diagrams, brand assets, style guides, or recordings.
- Research files, customer interviews, survey data, analytics, tickets, transcripts, or spreadsheets.
- Account, API, integration, MCP server, plugin, deployment, and repo workflow requirements.
- Open decisions, constraints, and approval preferences.
