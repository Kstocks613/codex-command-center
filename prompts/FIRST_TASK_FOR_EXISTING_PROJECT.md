Do not code yet.

Your first deliverables are:

1. `PROJECT_BRIEF.md`

4. `ENVIRONMENT_SETUP.md`
5. `AGENT_ROSTER.md`
6. `FIRST_SPRINT_PLAN.md`
7. `APPROVAL_POLICY.md`
8. `TASK_LOG.md`
9. `DECISION_LOG.md`

Then ask me the minimum questions needed before execution.

After I answer, propose the smallest useful first deliverable and ask for approval.
