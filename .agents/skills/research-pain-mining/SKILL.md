# Research Pain Mining Skill

## Purpose

Find real user pain before building.

## When to Use

Use for:

- Market scans
- Niche selection
- Product validation
- Customer research
- Content engine creation
- Checklist/product architecture work

## Operating Rules

1. Preserve exact buyer language where useful.
2. Separate observed pain from assumptions.
3. Rank pain by urgency, cost, frequency, and willingness to pay.
4. Convert pain into product requirements.
5. Do not summarize too early.

## Output Table

```markdown
| Source | URL | Buyer Type | Exact Quote | Pain Category | Mistake Feared | Decision Stage | Urgency | Possible Product Feature | Content Angle |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
```

## Failure Modes

- Guessing instead of researching.
- Building before pain is validated.
- Treating generic interest as demand.
